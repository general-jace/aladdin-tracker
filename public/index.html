Personal Portfolio + Institutional Footprint Monitor
What this tool can and can't do: Asset-class weights, drift, concentration, and adherence math below are exact given what you enter. Sharpe, Sortino, volatility, max drawdown, beta, and capture ratios are only computed — and are only as accurate — if you have a real periodic return series in the "Returns Data" tab; otherwise the report says so instead of guessing. Holdings prices can be refreshed live from Stooq (free EOD data). BlackRock revenue/net income/EPS can be auto-filled from SEC EDGAR's structured XBRL data. AUM, net inflows, and retention commentary are not published as structured data anywhere free or paid at reasonable cost — those stay manual entry with a required source + date. This is analysis of the data you provide, not financial advice.
